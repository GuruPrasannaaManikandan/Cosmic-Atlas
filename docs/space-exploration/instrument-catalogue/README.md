# Instrument Catalogue

Instruments are `components` of catalogue objects (`frontend/src/missions/catalog/*.ts`). Each has a description and source ids. In the app, the components become the parts you can focus on in the **Inspect** tab.

| Host | Instrument / component | What it did | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apollo 11 EASEP | Passive seismometer | Measured seismic activity and transmitted it to Earth (21 Jul – 27 Aug 1969) | NSSDCA 1969-059C |
| Apollo 11 EASEP | Lunar dust detector | Dust accumulation (experiment 1969-059C-05) | NSSDCA 1969-059C |
| Apollo 11 EASEP | Solar panels ×2, antenna mast | Power and communication | NSSDCA 1969-059C |
| Apollo 11 LRRR | Fused-silica corner-cube array; folding support | Laser ranging to Earth (PI C. O. Alley) | NSSDCA 1969-059C-04 |
| Apollo 11 LM | Descent engine (~45,000 N, deep-throttling); legs and footpads; ladder | Landing; launch platform for the ascent stage | NSSDCA 1969-059C |
| Opportunity | Pancam, Navcam, Mini-TES, Mössbauer spectrometer, APXS, Microscopic Imager, RAT | Imaging, mineralogy, rock abrasion | NASA Science |
| Chandrayaan-3 lander (ISRO) | RAMBHA-LP, ChaSTE, ILSA, LRA (NASA-provided, passive) | Plasma, thermophysics, seismic activity, laser ranging | ISRO |
| Chandrayaan-3 rover (ISRO) | APXS, LIBS | Elemental composition | ISRO |

**Phase 1b will add** the per-mission ALSEP instruments (Apollo 12, 14, 15, 16 and 17), each from its NSSDCA experiment record.
